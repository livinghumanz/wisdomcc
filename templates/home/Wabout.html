About Us

Established 20 June 2018.

For the comfort of students, the management has furnished the institute distinctively from other surrounding institutions — school-like infrastructure, hassle-free power supply, a first aid kit, drinking water, telephone facilities to reach parents, a photocopy machine and rest room facilities.

Wisdom takes every effort towards students' academic growth: classes all seven days of the week, unit-wise portion lecturing, subject-wise examinations, revised examination papers drawn for parental attention, parents' and teachers' meetings, and continuous tracking of each ward's academic performance.
