About Wisdom

A holistic education, in an ambiance built for learning.

Wisdom Coaching Centre (WCC) facilitates superior lecturing for all subjects of the school stream, plus training and soft-skills programmes for individuals looking for an upgraded grade at the institutions where they study. Throughout the year we offer continuing education courses, seminars and cycle-test based learning delivered by experienced faculty.

This sprawling institution provides a holistic education in an ambiance that makes no compromise on discipline, dedication and commitment. We ensure that students who pass out turn out to be academically brilliant, morally upright and empowered individuals.

What we offer

Courses & Services

From NEET preparation to spoken English, Abacus and daycare — everything under one roof.

NEET

NEET Crash Course & Repeaters

Forty days of intensive daily coaching across Physics, Chemistry, Botany and Zoology, with NEET-pattern tests throughout and a full revision track for repeaters.

Class 8–12

Regular School Coaching

Academic coaching across State Board, CBSE, ICSE and IGCSE curricula, including one-to-one sessions where a student needs focused attention.

Practice

Weekly Mock Tests

Ten mock tests under real exam conditions, building accuracy, confidence and time management before it matters.

VAC

Value-Added Courses

Spoken English, communication training and life skills that go beyond the syllabus — plus music, drawing and yoga as part of a rounded programme.

Language

Hindi Prachar Sabha & Spoken Hindi

Structured Hindi courses through Dakshin Bharat Hindi Prachar Sabha, alongside conversational spoken Hindi.

Skills

Abacus Training

Mental arithmetic that strengthens concentration, memory and problem-solving from a tender age.
